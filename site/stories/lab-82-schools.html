Laboratory equipment for 82 schools not delivered

In June 2024 the Ministry signed four contracts, worth K8,654,504.49 in total, to supply laboratory equipment and reagents to 82 schools within 12 to 16 weeks of the advance payment.

Advance payments of K2,169,546.90 were made in August and September 2024.

A records review and physical check of selected schools in May 2025 found the equipment and reagents 'had not been delivered to all the 82 schools'.

Auditor General, 2024 report, PDF page 302. Position as at May 2025; later delivery or recovery may not be reflected. An audit finding is a question, not proof of wrongdoing.
