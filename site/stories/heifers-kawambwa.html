818 heifers ordered for Kawambwa; 100 arrived

On 15 November 2024 the Zambia Correctional Service engaged three suppliers to deliver 818 yearling heifers to the Chishinga Livestock Breeding Centre in Kawambwa within 4 to 8 weeks, for K10,879,462.

Two suppliers were paid advances totalling K1,826,824.

As at 31 August 2025 one supplier had delivered 100 of its 273 heifers; the other two had delivered none, five months after the contracts expired. There was no evidence that liquidated damages had been claimed.

Auditor General, 2024 report, PDF pages 130-131. Position as at 31 August 2025; later delivery or recovery may not be reflected. An audit finding is a question, not proof of wrongdoing.
