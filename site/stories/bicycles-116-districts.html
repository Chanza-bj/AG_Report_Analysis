10,335 bicycles for cash-transfer districts not delivered

On 18 March 2024 the Ministry contracted Raton Freight Zambia Limited to deliver 10,335 bicycles to all 116 districts under the Social Cash Transfer programme by early June 2024, for K13,947,083.

An advance of K3,486,771 was paid on 4 April 2024.

As at 31 August 2025 no bicycles had been delivered. The Ministry said it had terminated the contract and asked the Attorney General to recover the money; the funds had not been recovered.

Auditor General, 2024 report, PDF page 148. Position as at 31 August 2025; later delivery or recovery may not be reflected. An audit finding is a question, not proof of wrongdoing.
