Schools' laboratory gas paid for in full, items missing

On 13 June 2024 the Ministry contracted Mecs Services Limited to supply laboratory gas cylinders, burners and fittings to schools for K918,511.

The supplier received a K229,628 advance in August 2024 and the K688,883 balance on 23 May 2025: the full contract sum.

A check of selected schools and their delivery records in April 2026 found 1,032 items worth K268,131 not delivered.

Auditor General, 2025 report, PDF pages 163-164. Position as at April 2026; later delivery or recovery may not be reflected. An audit finding is a question, not proof of wrongdoing.
