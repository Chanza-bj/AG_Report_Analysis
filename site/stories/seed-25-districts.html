Seed for 62,317 beneficiaries in 25 districts not delivered

On 28 November 2024 the Ministry contracted Trans Seed Zambia Limited to supply rice, bean and sunflower seed to all 116 districts for the rainfed cropping component of the 2024/25 season, for K31,564,860.

The supplier had been paid K9,785,106.

As at 31 August 2025 the seed had not been delivered. Visits to selected districts found seed costing K12,183,482, meant for 62,317 beneficiaries in 25 districts, not delivered.

Auditor General, 2024 report, PDF pages 137-138. Position as at 31 August 2025; later delivery or recovery may not be reflected. An audit finding is a question, not proof of wrongdoing.
