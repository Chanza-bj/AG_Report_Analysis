Serenje early-childhood centre left unfinished

On 18 April 2023 the Ministry signed a contract with Pozzolana Engineers and Builders Limited for 20 early childhood education hubs, including the Kanona Primary hub at K3,864,753, to be finished within 12 months of site handover.

Payments of K2,268,039 (58.68% of the Kanona contract sum) had been made by 31 December 2025.

A site visit in May 2026 found the contractor had abandoned the site in 2024; the buildings were roofed but plumbing and electrical work were unfinished. The contract had been extended four times, the last in April 2026.

Auditor General, 2025 report, PDF page 154. Position as at May 2026; later delivery or recovery may not be reflected. An audit finding is a question, not proof of wrongdoing.
