How to read these numbers fairly

  • A finding is a question, not a verdict. Say "the report records K3.56bn in unpaid obligations," not "the ministry lost it."
  • Not every year lines up. The budget table is missing for 2021; a single findings total exists only for 2022 and 2023; the 2024 and 2025 reports list examples instead of one total.
  • One huge line (tax arrears) distorts the findings totals, so it is shown on its own.
  • Kwacha and US dollar amounts are never added together.
  • The contracts list is a first set, drawn from highlighted cases, not every contract in the reports.
  • These figures come from the published main reports only. Ministries may since have given explanations, recorded in later reports and parliamentary committees.
