Zambia's public money, 2021-2025

A short brief from the Auditor General's Reports on the Accounts of the Republic. Every figure is traceable to a report year and page. Interactive dashboard and full data: the dashboard.

Read this first. An audit finding is a question the auditors could not close, not a proven crime. Phrase figures as "the report records K X" or "the auditors flagged", not "the ministry stole K X". Where years are not comparable, that is stated.

The headline numbers

  1. Spending is closer to the budget. Payments rose from 76% of the final budget in 2022 to 86% in 2025 (88% in 2023, 82% in 2024). 2021 has no comparable statement.
  2. The surplus has nearly gone. Money collected minus money spent fell from about K30.2 billion in 2022 to about K2.1 billion in 2025.
  3. The accounts got a clean opinion every year. An unqualified opinion for 2022-2025 (2021 had none). A clean opinion covers the consolidated accounts overall; it does not certify every transaction, which is why large findings sit alongside it.
  4. Flagged findings fell, but are comparable only for 2021-2023 (K133.3bn, K103.5bn, K81.5bn), dominated by one externally sourced tax-arrears line. 2024 and 2025 publish no comparable total.
  5. The same problems and ministries recur. 26 of 39 finding categories appear every comparable year; four rose every single year, including unpaid bills (K3.9bn to K5.2bn). Nine ministries appear in all five reports. Unresolved-findings paragraphs fell from 40 (2022) to 27 (2025).
  6. Ministries were underfunded by billions. In-report figures show at least K47.7 billion in underfunding over 2023-2025 — Agriculture about 40% unfunded in 2025, Health underfunded by K3.09 billion, linked to 15 budgeted activities being dropped.

Contracts the auditors questioned

From 34 named contracts deep-checked against their pages:

  • ZTE — Safe City security network, US$210 million, with US$178.5 million in loans still outstanding (2023).
  • Seed suppliers (FISP), 2022: only 28% of 33,283 tonnes delivered on time; K786 million paid before delivery to 13 suppliers yet to deliver at the 15 January deadline; a later check found most delivered between mid-January and March 2023.
  • AVIC (Cancer Centre Ndola, K306m) and Siemens (Cancer Hospital Lusaka, US$30.8m) — hospital builds delayed; an irregular US$6.6m advance to Siemens without verifying the guarantee.
  • Pozzolana — ECE hub, Serenje: contractor abandoned the site; 0 of 40 early-childhood hubs built.
  • Menov Trading — pontoon materials: K1.84m paid before the contract was signed.

Limits of this analysis

  • Findings are unresolved questions at the report's cut-off, not proven losses.
  • The budget-execution and single findings-total series do not cover every year.
  • Kwacha and US dollar amounts are never added together.
  • The 34 contracts are the deep-verified set; the reports name about 182 priced contracts in all, flagged verified / unverified in the dashboard.
  • Materiality (underfunding) figures are indicative in-report figures, not full appropriations.

How to use and cite

Cite any figure with the report year and page, e.g. Auditor General, 2025 report, page 5. The repository is version-tagged (v1.1.0); corrections appear as new versions. Before publishing names, give institutions a right of reply, and consider sharing a draft with a parliamentary analyst or Transparency International Zambia.
