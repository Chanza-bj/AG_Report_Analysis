# Zambia's public money, 2021-2025: key findings brief

*A short, shareable summary built from the Auditor General's Reports on the Accounts of the Republic, 2021-2025. Contract figures cite the report year and PDF page; budget and findings figures cite the report year. Interactive dashboard and full data: https://chanza-bj.github.io/AG_Report_Analysis/*

**Read this first.** An audit finding is a question the auditors could not close, not a proven crime. Phrase figures as "the report records K X" or "the auditors flagged", not "the ministry stole K X". Where years are not comparable, that is stated.

## The headline numbers

1. **Spending is closer to the budget.** Payments rose from **76% of the final budget in 2022 to 86% in 2025** (not a straight line: 88% in 2023, 82% in 2024). 2021 has no comparable statement.

2. **The surplus has nearly gone.** Money collected minus money spent fell from about **K30.2 billion in 2022 to about K2.1 billion in 2025**.

3. **The financial statements got a clean opinion every year.** The Auditor General issued an **unqualified opinion for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025** (2021 had no formal opinion section). A clean opinion covers the consolidated accounts overall; it does not certify every transaction, which is why large findings sit alongside it.

4. **The value of flagged findings fell between the two years with a comparable total**, from K103.5bn in 2022 to K81.5bn in 2023, and is dominated by one externally sourced tax-arrears line. The other years publish no comparable single total.

5. **The same problems and ministries recur.** **26 of 39 finding categories appear every comparable year**; four rose every single year, including **unpaid bills (K3.9bn to K5.2bn)**. **Nine ministries appear in all five reports.** Unresolved-findings paragraphs fell from 40 (2022) to 27 (2025).

6. **Ministries were underfunded by billions.** In-report figures show at least **K47.7 billion** in underfunding across ministries over 2023-2025 - for example Agriculture about **40% unfunded in 2025**, and Health underfunded by **K3.09 billion in 2025**, which the report links to 15 budgeted activities being dropped.

## Contracts the auditors questioned (verified sample)

From 34 named contracts deep-checked against their pages, examples:
- **ZTE - Safe City security network, US$210 million**, with **US$178.5 million in loans still outstanding** (2023).
- **Seed suppliers (FISP), 2022:** only 28% of 33,283 tonnes delivered on time; **K786 million paid before delivery to 13 suppliers yet to deliver** at the 15 January deadline. The Office's July 2023 verification found most delivered between mid-January and March 2023, after the planting window (2022 report, page 215).
- **AVIC (Cancer Centre Ndola, K306m)** and **Siemens (Cancer Hospital Lusaka, US$30.8m)** - hospital builds delayed; an **irregular US$6.6m advance** paid to Siemens without verifying the guarantee.
- **Pozzolana - Kanona ECE hub, Serenje:** 58.68% of the K3.86m contract paid; a May 2026 site visit found the contractor had **abandoned the site** in 2024 (2025 report, page 154).
- **Menov Trading - pontoon materials:** K1.84m **paid before the contract was signed**.

Recurring suppliers (AVIC, ZTE, Jiangsu Gold, Nyiombo) each appear across multiple years' reports.

## Limits of this analysis
- Findings are unresolved questions at the report's cut-off, not proven losses.
- The budget-execution and single findings-total series do not cover every year.
- Kwacha and US dollar amounts are never added together.
- The 34 contracts are the deep-verified set; the reports name about 182 priced contracts in all, shown with a verified / unverified flag in the dashboard.
- Materiality (underfunding) figures are indicative in-report figures, not full appropriations.

## How to use and cite
Cite any figure with the report year and page, e.g. *Auditor General, 2025 report, page 5*. Every correction is logged as a new version in the repository changelog. Before publishing names, give the institutions a right of reply, and consider sharing a draft with a parliamentary analyst or Transparency International Zambia.
