# Questions these data cannot answer

These reports and datasets record what the Office of the Auditor General published in its main reports. The following questions need other sources.

## Resolution and consequences
1. Were the findings later resolved? The reports list matters unresolved at a cut-off date. Outcomes appear in later Treasury Minutes (Action Taken Reports) and Public Accounts Committee proceedings, which are not in these datasets.
2. Did any finding lead to recovery of funds, surcharge, disciplinary action, or prosecution? The reports do not track outcomes.
3. How many findings are repeats of prior-year matters versus new matters for the same ministry?

## Value by ministry
4. What is the monetary value of findings for each ministry? The comparable category table is not broken down by Head, and the paragraph counts carry no amounts. Value by ministry would require reading and totalling each paragraph in the body.
5. How large are the findings relative to each ministry's budget? This needs the Estimates of Revenue and Expenditure (appropriations) to compute findings as a share of funds managed.

## Comparability and scope
6. Why did the 2024 and 2025 reports drop the comparative "Trend Analysis" table, and what would their comparable category totals be?
7. Does the fall in reported totals reflect fewer irregularities, or a narrower scope, changed definitions, or fewer entities audited each year?
8. What is the source and basis of the large 2021 "Over Commitment" (K16.86 billion) and "Uncompetitive Procurement" (K12.83 billion) figures, which fall to near zero in 2022?
9. Are the same underlying transactions counted under more than one query category, or across more than one ministry?

## Currency and definitions
10. What is the Kwacha value of the US dollar findings in the narrative, and on what exchange basis? These are excluded from the Kwacha totals here.
11. How does the Office define each query category, and did any definition change between 2021 and 2025?

## Context
12. How do Zambia's findings compare with peer Supreme Audit Institutions on a like basis?
13. What audit opinion did the Office issue on the financial statements each year, and did it change? (Recorded in each report's "Audit Opinion on the Accounts" section, not yet extracted here.)
